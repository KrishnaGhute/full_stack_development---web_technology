The Secret to Perfectly Grilled Chicken

Marination and technique are everything. Follow these steps for juicy, tender chicken.

Marination Tips

Use yogurt, lemon, ginger-garlic paste, Kashmiri chili, and garam masala. Marinate at least 4 hours or overnight for best results.

Grill Temperature

Sear on high heat for a crust, then move to medium heat and cook through. Aim for internal temp ~74°C (165°F).

Finishing Touches

Let the chicken rest for 5 minutes, garnish with lemon and coriander, and serve hot.
