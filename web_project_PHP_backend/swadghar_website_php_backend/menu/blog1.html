5 Healthy Recipes for Busy Weekdays

Quick, nutritious, and family-friendly meals you can prepare in under 30 minutes.

Sprout Salad

Mix boiled sprouts with chopped tomato, cucumber, lemon and chaat masala. Toss and serve chilled.

Moong Dal Khichdi

Cook moong dal with rice and turmeric; temper with ghee, cumin and curry leaves for a comforting meal.

Oats Upma

Roast oats and stir-fry with veggies and mustard seeds; finish with lemon juice and fresh cilantro.

Vegetable Poha

Sauté onions, mustard seeds and chopped vegetables, then add soaked poha and cook with turmeric and lemon.

Roti Wraps

Fill whole wheat rotis with leftover sabzi, chutney and greens for a portable lunch option.
