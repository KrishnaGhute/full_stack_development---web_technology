About Swaad Ghar

Swaad Ghar is a family-run kitchen serving home-cooked Indian food made from authentic recipes, fresh ingredients, and generations of love. We bring traditional flavours to your table — think warm spices, slow-simmered gravies, and the comfort of a family kitchen.

Our Journey

What started in 1998 as a small home kitchen run by the Sharma family soon became a neighbourhood favourite. Over the years we kept one promise — to preserve authentic recipes and cook with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients. From festival dinners shared with neighbours to the first organised catering order, each milestone shaped the Swaad Ghar you see today.

Fun anecdote: Our earliest customers would line up at the door for our paneer butter masala. One neighbour once traded a jar of homemade mango pickle for an entire meal — and both recipes are still cherished in the family.

Meet the Family

At the heart of Swaad Ghar is our family kitchen. We’re a small team of home cooks who believe in slow cooking, careful seasoning and sharing food that feels like a hug.

Our Signature Recipes

We focus on authentic recipes you can trust — every dish is prepared the family way. A few favourites:

Paneer Butter Masala

Soft paneer cubes in a rich tomato-cream gravy spiced with kasuri methi and a hint of garam masala.

Chicken Biryani

Aromatic basmati rice layered with marinated chicken, fried onions and whole spices — slow-cooked to perfection.

Gulab Jamun

Soft milk dumplings soaked in saffron-cardamom syrup — a sweet finish that feels like home.
